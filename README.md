# The Dyon Constitution

**Departement-Of-Dyon — Founding Document**

This Constitution establishes the supreme law of the Departement-Of-Dyon.
All statutes, decrees, and customs derive their authority from this document.

## Article I — Sovereignty
The Departement-Of-Dyon is the sole and final authority over itself. No
external jurisdiction shall override its decrees within its own domain.

## Article II — The Laws
The laws of the Departement are codified in the repositories of this
organization. Each law is supreme within its domain; where laws conflict,
the older law prevails unless expressly amended.

## Article III — Amendments
Any law may be amended by a majority of the Keepers of the Code. Silent
acquiescence for seven days constitutes ratification.

## Article IV — Enforcement
Enforcement is at the discretion of the Departement. Unenforced laws remain
law; awareness of a law creates obligation.

---
*Ratified at the founding of the Departement-Of-Dyon.*
